How to Download Twitter Videos on Android

No app to install. TwitterDown works in Chrome on Android — paste the link and save the MP4.

Steps

  1. Open the X app, find the video, tap Share, then Copy link. (On x.com in Chrome, copy the link from the address bar.)
  2. Open TwitterDown in Chrome and paste the link in the box. Tap Download.
  3. Pick a quality and tap it. The file downloads and lands in your Downloads folder — open it in Gallery or Files.

Tips

If Chrome asks where to save, choose Downloads. Only public posts work. If a video will not download, check the link is from x.com or twitter.com — TwitterDown is X-only and will not work with TikTok, YouTube or Instagram links.

Photos work the same way — paste a photo post link and each photo gets its own preview and download button, at original quality.
