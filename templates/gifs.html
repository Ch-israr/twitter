How to Download Twitter GIFs from X (Twitter)

On X, a GIF is really a short looping video, so it downloads as an MP4 file — which plays everywhere and is easy to share.

Steps

  1. Find the GIF on X, tap or click Share, then Copy link (or copy the link from the address bar).
  2. Paste the link into TwitterDown and tap Download.
  3. Pick a quality and save the MP4. That is the GIF, as a video file.

Tips

The MP4 will loop in most players if you set it to repeat, and you can share it like any video. Only public posts work. If you need a true .gif file, convert the MP4 with any free video-to-GIF converter after downloading.

The same steps work for photos too — paste a photo post link and each photo gets its own preview and download button, at original quality.
