How to Download Twitter Videos on iPhone

You do not need an app. TwitterDown works in Safari on your iPhone — paste the link, choose a quality, and the MP4 saves to your phone.

Steps

  1. Open the X app, find the video, tap Share, then Copy link. (In Safari on x.com, copy the link from the address bar.)
  2. Open TwitterDown in Safari and paste the link in the box. Tap Download.
  3. Pick a quality — 720p is best if you want to share it again, 480p is smaller. The file downloads through TwitterDown and saves to your Downloads folder in Files.
  4. To move it to Photos: open Files → Downloads, tap the video, tap Share → Save Video.

Tips

Only public posts work — private posts and posts from protected accounts will not download. If a download opens a player instead of saving, long-press the quality button and choose Download Linked File. Also works on iPad the same way.

Photos work the same way — paste a photo post link and each photo gets its own preview and download button, at original quality.
