How to Download Twitter Videos on PC

No software to install. TwitterDown works in any browser on Windows or Mac.

Steps

  1. Open the post on x.com, copy the link from the address bar — or click Share on the post, then Copy link.
  2. Open TwitterDown, paste the link in the box, and click Download.
  3. Pick a quality — 720p for the sharpest copy, 480p or 320p for a smaller file. Clicking saves the MP4 through TwitterDown to your Downloads folder.

Tips

Right-click a quality button and choose "Save link as" if your browser opens the player instead of downloading. Only public posts work, and only X links — TwitterDown does not work with TikTok, YouTube or Instagram.

Photos work the same way — paste a photo post link and each photo gets its own preview and download button, at original quality.
