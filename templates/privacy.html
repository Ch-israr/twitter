Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

TwitterDown ("we", "us", "our") is a free online tool for downloading videos and GIFs from X (Twitter) as MP4 files. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, https://twitter-7vlf.onrender.com/, and how we use it.

Information we collect

We do not ask you for your name, email address, or any account details, and you do not need an account to use TwitterDown. When you paste a link, we fetch the video information from X so we can show you the available download options. We do not store the links you paste, and we do not store the videos you download. Downloads are streamed through our server and are not saved on it.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google Tag Manager on this website. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices. You can also control cookies through your browser settings.

Log data

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically collect standard log information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit, for security and to keep the service running. We do not use this information to identify you personally.

Links to other websites

TwitterDown links to X (Twitter) when you download a video. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of X or any other third-party website.

Children's privacy

TwitterDown is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us through the Contact page.
